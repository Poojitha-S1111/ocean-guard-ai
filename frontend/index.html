Forensic Methodology

The 6-Stage Marine Pollution Pipeline

From raw satellite backscatter to probabilistic candidate vessel investigation.

01

SAR Speckle Damping

Lee adaptive filter and multi-scale normalization eliminate Rayleigh speckle noise while preserving sharp edge boundaries of hydrocarbon damping zones.

cv2 + Lee Filter
02

Dark-Patch Extraction

Dynamic ocean masking and adaptive thresholding isolate areas where capillary and short gravity waves are depressed by viscoelastic surfactant films.

Contour Segmentation
03

ML Patch Classification

Extracts 9 morphological and GLCM texture features (elongation, contrast, energy). Scikit-learn Random Forest model calculates likelihood score vs biogenic look-alikes.

Random Forest (Scikit-Learn)
04

Hydrodynamic Drift Backtracking

Inverts standard oceanographic drift: Net Vector = Ocean Current + ~3% Wind Speed. Reverses vectors hour-by-hour to derive the past trajectory and expanding source region.

NOAA GNOME Approximation
05

Candidate Vessel Ranking

Fuses sample AIS tracks across 4 configurable sub-scores: Timing window, Haversine proximity, Trajectory traversal, and Drift-path alignment. Never identifies a "culprit".

Multivariate Scoring
06

Historical Correlation & Timeline

Cross-references spatial coordinates against SQLite archives to identify chronic illegal discharge hotspots, recurring corridors, and prior environmental baseline incidents.

SQLite Forensic Ledger

⚠️

Operational Scope & Decision-Support Limitations

OceanGuard AI is strictly an investigative decision-support system. Candidate vessel rankings reflect spatial and temporal correlation with backtracked drift envelopes, not physical proof of responsibility.

  • Natural Look-alikes: Low-wind calm zones (< 3 m/s), biogenic algal surfactants, internal waves, and rain squalls can mimic oil slick damping.
  • AIS Gaps: Vessels can intentionally or unintentionally deactivate AIS transponders (dark vessels).
  • Ground Truthing: Satellite leads must be corroborated with maritime patrol aircraft (SLAR/IR), vessel logbook inspections, and chemical oil fingerprinting.
